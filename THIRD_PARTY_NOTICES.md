## SCOWL English word data

Neokey's generated bilingual English lexicon contains a filtered word list
derived from SCOWL release `2020.12.07`, commit
`5ef55f9c42730ebe4394a78b77855468a6f15dd2`.

Source: <https://github.com/en-wl/wordlist/tree/rel-2020.12.07>

The size-10 American English list supplies the Common tier. Additional
size-20 words are included only when replay through Neokey with bilingual
protection disabled changes the typed word. Tokens are normalized to
lowercase ASCII, restricted to 2-20 letters, deduplicated, and packed into a
generated C++ lookup table.
